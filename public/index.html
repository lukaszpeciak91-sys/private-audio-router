Puzru Privacy Policy

Developer and publisher: Napahu Studios

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Puzru does not require an account or sign-in and does not request microphone permission. It does not capture or record microphone audio, or record or store your conversations or their audio content.

Puzru uses Android audio-system information needed for routing and diagnostics. Diagnostics may inspect technical state and metadata exposed by public Android APIs, including playback and recording-session metadata. Recording-session metadata is technical audio-system information; Puzru does not capture the microphone audio associated with those sessions. Diagnostic information may include app and Android versions, device and model information, app process and state information, available and current audio devices, public playback and recording configuration metadata, and routing attempts, results, timing, and technical events.

App preferences are stored locally in Puzru’s app-private storage. Diagnostic information is generated and processed on your device and can exist in the running app’s local state. Reports are not automatically saved or sent. A persistent report is created outside Puzru’s app-private storage only when you explicitly choose Save diagnostic report and select a destination through Android. After export, the selected location or service—not Puzru—controls that copy’s handling, retention, and deletion.

The current version does not request Android’s Internet permission, has no Puzru backend or network transmission path, and does not include analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services or SDKs. It does not currently send diagnostic reports to the developer or a Puzru server, so Puzru has no server-side diagnostic retention.

App-owned data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. Local preferences remain in app-private storage until they are changed or the app’s data is removed; transient diagnostic state is not a saved report. Exported reports remain under the controls of the location or service you selected.

Puzru Privacy Policy

Developer and publisher: Napahu Studios

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Puzru does not require an account or sign-in and does not request microphone permission. It does not capture or record microphone audio, or record or store your conversations or their audio content.

Puzru uses Android audio-system information needed for routing and diagnostics. Diagnostics may inspect technical state and metadata exposed by public Android APIs, including playback and recording-session metadata. Recording-session metadata is technical audio-system information; Puzru does not capture the microphone audio associated with those sessions. A diagnostic report may include its timestamp; Puzru package, version, process, controller, permission, and state information; Android version, API level, security patch, and build identifiers; device manufacturer, model, and product; available and current audio devices and audio mode; public playback and recording configuration metadata; and bounded histories of routing attempts, results, timing, and technical playback, recording, and lifecycle events.

App preferences are stored locally in Puzru’s app-private storage. Diagnostic information is generated and processed on your device and can exist in the running app’s local state. Reports are not automatically saved or transmitted. Save diagnostic report creates a persistent copy only when you explicitly choose it and select a destination through Android’s document picker. Send diagnostic report instead creates a temporary text attachment in Puzru’s app-private cache and hands it, through Android’s chooser, to the external app you select.

The current version does not request Android’s Internet permission, has no Puzru backend or network transmission path, and does not include analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services or SDKs. Puzru itself does not automatically send diagnostic reports. When you explicitly choose Send diagnostic report, Puzru prepares the report locally and hands the attachment to the external app you select; the Napahu Studios privacy and support address may be prefilled. Any actual transmission is performed by the selected external app or service after your action. Copies handled by that destination or service are governed by its privacy practices and controls, not Puzru’s.

If you choose to send a diagnostic report to Napahu Studios, Napahu Studios and its email service provider may receive the attached technical report, the sender email address, and any message you add. Napahu Studios uses this information only to respond to your support request and investigate or troubleshoot Puzru. Napahu Studios retains information it controls only for as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes or to comply with applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion of copies controlled by Napahu Studios by contacting napahustudios@gmail.com. Copies controlled by an email or other external service remain subject to that service’s privacy practices and controls.

Diagnostic share attachments are created in Puzru’s app-private cache. When preparing a new attachment, Puzru removes prior regular report files in its diagnostic-share cache where deletion succeeds; no fixed deletion time for the current attachment is guaranteed. App-owned data, including preferences and cached reports, is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. Local preferences remain in app-private storage until they are changed or the app’s data is removed. Persistent saved reports and copies handled by a selected external app or service remain under the controls of their selected location, app, or service.
